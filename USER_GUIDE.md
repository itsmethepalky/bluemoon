# The Blue Moon — User Guide

## 1. Login

Open the application and sign in using your account.

The system supports:

- Admin
- Staff
- Customer

Available screens depend on the user's role.

## 2. Dashboard

The dashboard provides a quick view of business activity, including:

- Today's sales
- Monthly sales
- Low-stock products
- Sales trends
- Top products
- Inventory value
- Estimated profit

## 3. Products

Products can be managed with:

- Product name
- SKU/code
- Category
- Brand
- Purchase price
- Selling price
- Stock quantity
- Reorder level
- Active/inactive status

Use the active/inactive option for soft deletion where appropriate.

## 4. POS

Typical sales workflow:

```text
Select customer (if required)
        ↓
Add products to cart
        ↓
Adjust quantities
        ↓
Apply discount/tax if applicable
        ↓
Select payment method
        ↓
Complete sale
        ↓
Inventory is updated
```

Supported payment methods include cash, card, mobile payment and credit.

## 5. Purchases

Create a purchase by:

1. Selecting a supplier.
2. Adding products.
3. Entering quantities and purchase prices.
4. Saving the purchase.

Completed purchases increase inventory.

## 6. Customers

Customer accounts can contain:

- Contact information
- Purchase history
- Credit limit
- Current credit balance
- Recorded payments

## 7. Credit

A credit sale increases the customer's outstanding balance.

When the customer pays:

1. Open the customer's account.
2. Record the payment.
3. Confirm the amount.
4. Verify the updated balance.

## 8. Returns

Returns should reference the original sale where supported.

A return can result in:

- Inventory adjustment
- Sales/financial adjustment

Always verify the returned quantity before confirming the operation.

## 9. Suppliers

Supplier records can be used when creating purchases and maintaining purchasing history.

## 10. Reports

Reports help review:

- Sales
- Inventory
- Top products
- Low-stock products
- Category inventory value
- Estimated profit

## 11. Staff accounts

Admin users can manage staff accounts and roles.

Do not give administrator privileges to users who do not require them.

## 12. Security best practices

- Use strong passwords.
- Do not share accounts.
- Do not expose service-role credentials.
- Log out from shared computers.
- Give staff the minimum required role.
- Keep the application and dependencies updated.
