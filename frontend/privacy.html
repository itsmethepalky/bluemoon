Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

1. About The Blue Moon

The Blue Moon is a small-business management and point-of-sale system providing product and inventory management, purchasing, supplier management, customer management, store-credit management, point-of-sale billing, returns, and business reports.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the application may process, how that information is used, and how it is protected.

2. Information We Process

Depending on how the application is used, The Blue Moon may process the following categories of information:

Account information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number, when provided
  • User role and account status
  • Authentication-related account information

Business and customer information

  • Customer names and contact information
  • Supplier information
  • Product and inventory information
  • Purchase records
  • Sales and invoice records
  • Store-credit limits and balances
  • Credit-payment records
  • Product-return records
  • Business reporting information

3. How Information Is Used

Information processed by The Blue Moon is used to provide and maintain the application's business-management functions, including:

  • Authenticating users
  • Managing accounts, roles, and permissions
  • Managing products and inventory
  • Processing sales and purchases
  • Managing customers and suppliers
  • Managing store credit and payments
  • Processing returns
  • Generating sales, inventory, and business reports
  • Maintaining business records
  • Protecting the application and its data

4. Authentication

The Blue Moon uses Supabase Auth for account registration, login, and authentication.

After authentication, the application uses authenticated access tokens to identify users when communicating with the application backend.

Application roles and permissions are maintained by the application's backend and database. Administrative and staff functionality is restricted according to those permissions.

5. Data Storage and Service Providers

The Blue Moon uses third-party infrastructure to operate its application and store data.

  • Supabase provides authentication and hosted PostgreSQL database infrastructure.
  • Render provides application hosting.
  • jsDelivr delivers selected frontend libraries used by the application.
  • Google Fonts provides fonts used by the application interface.

These services may process information as necessary to provide the services used by the application and are subject to their own terms and privacy policies.

6. Business Records

Sales, purchases, inventory, customer accounts, credit balances, supplier records, and related business information may be retained as part of normal business operations.

Authorized administrators are responsible for managing business records within the application.

Deactivating or deleting an account does not necessarily mean that historical business transactions are immediately deleted, particularly where those records are required for operational, inventory, reporting, or accounting purposes.

7. Access and Permissions

The Blue Moon uses role-based access controls.

Depending on their assigned role, users may have different levels of access to information and functionality.

Administrators can manage staff accounts and permissions. Staff users can access business-management functions permitted by their role. Customers can access information associated with their own customer account where that functionality is available.

Users should keep their passwords, authentication credentials, and access tokens confidential.

8. Security

The application uses technical and organizational measures intended to protect information, including:

  • Supabase authentication
  • Role-based authorization
  • Server-side authorization checks
  • Database access controls
  • Row Level Security at the database layer
  • HTTPS when provided by the hosting infrastructure
  • Security-related HTTP response headers
  • Content Security Policy
  • Subresource Integrity for selected third-party resources
  • Restricted cross-origin access
No method of transmitting or storing information over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should also take reasonable steps to protect their own account credentials and devices.

9. Cookies and Browser Storage

The Blue Moon does not intentionally use advertising cookies.

Authentication and application functionality may use browser storage or mechanisms provided by Supabase and the web browser to maintain an authenticated session.

Disabling required browser storage may prevent authentication or other application functionality from operating correctly.

10. Information Sharing

The Blue Moon does not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by infrastructure and service providers used to operate the application, including authentication, database, hosting, and content-delivery providers.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or where reasonably necessary to protect the security and integrity of the application.

11. Data Access, Correction, and Deletion Requests

If you use The Blue Moon through a business and want to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information associated with your account, please contact the administrator or business operating your account.

Because The Blue Moon can be used by individual businesses, the business operating the relevant system may be responsible for determining how customer and business records are managed.

12. Children's Information

The Blue Moon is designed for business and point-of-sale use and is not specifically directed toward children.

The application should not be used to knowingly collect unnecessary personal information from children.

13. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's functionality, infrastructure, or data practices change.

The current version will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

14. Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning an account or business using The Blue Moon, please contact the administrator or business operating the relevant The Blue Moon installation.
